The Menu

Grooming, done with patience

Every service is unhurried and adapted to your pet's coat, age and temperament. Anxious pets are our specialty.

🛁

Full Groom

Bath, haircut, blow-dry, nail trim and ear cleaning — the complete spa package, tailored to breed standards or your own style.

🫧

Bath & Brush

Deep-clean bath with quality shampoo and conditioner, full blow-dry and brush-out. Coats come home soft and shining.

✂️

Breed Cuts

From poodles to terriers — breed-standard trims or a custom look you love, cut clean and sharp, exactly to your style.

🐾

Nail & Paw Care

Nail trims, paw tidy-ups and pad care, done gently. Small service, big relief for you and your floors.

🌬️

De-shedding

Undercoat removal that dramatically cuts shedding at home. Recommended before Brownsville's coat-blowing seasons.

❤️

Senior & Anxious Pets

Extra time, quiet handling and breaks as needed. Nervous first-timers leave with their tails high.
